# E1 — BubR1 stability atlas: is the child's missense unstable or inactive? — submission note

**Standing:** written 2026-09-24 from entry [149](../methodology/149-the-stability-atlas-and-the-childs-missense.md);
re-check before the submission is assembled.

## The finding, in one sentence

The child's BUB1B missense allele is predicted **destabilising** (ThermoMPNN ΔΔG at the 87th
percentile of all 19,950 possible BubR1 substitutions, buried, in the pseudokinase domain), but
the same predictor **cannot separate** the published abundance-lowering from stable MVA
alleles (AUROC 0.60, 5 vs 3 alleles), so "unstable or inactive?" stays a prior-backed
prediction, not a result — and D3 remains a dosage argument.

## Limitations and what would change the conclusion

- A measured BubR1 protein level (Western, cycloheximide chase) in the child's cells settles
  the class directly and overrides every number here.
- ≥ 4 more MVA missense alleles with protein-level data (especially stable ones) would give the
  H083 test power; a second stability predictor (RaSP) or a BUB3/CDC20 complex model might
  separate Y155C/Q921H from the abundance-lowering set.
- If the missense proved function-impairing, D3 would weaken to "more of a partly working
  protein" and D5 (senolytic / mTOR, downstream of low BubR1 function) would gain relative weight.

## For the main thread

**Suggested evidence rows** (no position, gene + consequence only):

- H083, `refutes`, source entry 149: "Over 8 published MVA missense alleles of BUB1B with a
  protein-level readout (5 abundance-lowering, 3 stable; PMID 20516114), ThermoMPNN ΔΔG ranks
  abundance-lowering above stable with AUROC 0.60 (bootstrap 95 % CI 0.13–1.00); ≤ 0.6 and < 4
  stable alleles, so the pre-registered test is refuted and has no power."
- H083, `neutral`, source entry 149: "Adding one non-MVA stable allele (PMID 27239782) gives
  AUROC 0.70 (0.25–1.00); a sequence-only score (ESM1b) gives 0.53 on the MVA set."
- H084, `supports` (weak), source entry 149: "The child's BUB1B missense has ThermoMPNN ΔΔG at
  the 87th percentile of all 19,950 possible BubR1 substitutions (74th among folded residues),
  buried and in the UniProt pseudokinase domain."
- H084, `neutral`, source entry 149: "The E1 classifier calls the child's missense
  abundance-lowering with p = 0.64, equal to the class base rate (0.625); its leave-one-out
  AUROC is 0.13, so the call carries no information beyond the prior."

**Suggested verdicts:** H083 → **refuted** (no power; state both conditions). H084 →
**supported, weak** (commit a confidence only a little above the prior; the ΔΔG half is
evidence, the classifier half is not).

**Dictionary terms to add or improve** (only ΔΔG exists, at `docs/dictionary.md` line ~1170, without sign, example or context — improve it, do not duplicate):

- **ΔΔG (delta-delta-G)** (improve) — how much a substitution changes a protein's folding free energy,
  kcal/mol; here + = destabilising. *Example:* the published L1012P scores 3.1. *Context:*
  ThermoMPNN, entry 149; related: stability, ThermoMPNN.
- **ThermoMPNN** — a graph neural network that predicts ΔΔG for every substitution of a
  structure in one pass (PMID 38285937). *Example:* 21,000 BubR1 swaps in 12 s on a T4.
  *Context:* lane `thermompnn`, entry 149.
- **RSA (relative solvent accessibility)** — fraction of a residue's surface water can reach, 0
  buried → 1 exposed. *Example:* R550Q's residue 0.80. *Context:* Shrake–Rupley, entry 149.
- **Shrake–Rupley** — computes surface area by scattering test dots on each atom's sphere and
  counting the ones no neighbour covers. *Context:* `variant_atlas_sources.shrake_rupley`.
- **Variant atlas** — one row per possible substitution of a protein with its predicted effect
  and context. *Example:* BubR1, 19,950 rows. *Context:* `tools/variant_atlas.py`, entry 149.
- **Abundance-lowering vs function-impairing allele** — a missense that makes the cell degrade
  the protein (less of it) vs one that leaves the amount normal but breaks a job. *Example:*
  I909T vs Y155C (PMID 20516114). *Context:* H083/H084, D3.
- **Site-saturation mutagenesis (SSM)** — trying every amino acid at every position. *Context:*
  ThermoMPNN's scan mode, entry 149.
- **Leave-one-out (LOO)** — fit on all but one example, score that one, repeat for each.
  *Context:* entry 149 (and its small-n pooling artefact).
