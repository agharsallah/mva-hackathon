# E3 — LINCS signature reversal of BUB1B loss: submission note

**Standing:** written 2026-09-25 from entry 153; re-check before the submission is assembled.

## The finding, in one sentence

The LINCS BUB1B-knockout consensus signature hits its target (BUB1B is its most-lowered gene),
but it does not find other checkpoint-gene losses better than random gene sets. The
registered positive control fails, so H092 is **uninterpretable**. Phenytoin and albendazole
sit in both engines' top 100 reversers and in no decoy's top 100; that does not count as support.

## Limitations and what would change the conclusion

A control that passes against non-checkpoint essential-gene knockouts would make the run
interpretable, but it needs a new claim. A BUB1B signature from muscle or RMS cells, or from a
hypomorph rather than a knockout, could differ. With the current control, a pass would still
leave a 10–20 % chance that the pass came from chance.
