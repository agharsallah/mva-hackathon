Step 5 — what the literature knows, and what that is worth

A literature route is the easiest route in a rare-disease pipeline to fool yourself with: the gene at the top of any search is the gene most has been written about. So this step asks three knowledge questions — has anyone published about the alleles this child carries, does any single paper discuss a candidate gene alongside several of the child's features, and does a leading missense allele land in an annotated protein domain — and then asks a fourth that judges all three: does any of it order anything that a raw publication count does not?
