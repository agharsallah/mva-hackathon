  • If unstable the protein folds and is then thrown away — the job is to keep it, by shielding it from degradation.
  • If inactive raising the amount of a dead enzyme buys little — the job is to clean up the senescent cells it leaves behind.
We did not hide the weak link — we tested it
In-gene calibration on n = 8 MVA alleles reached only AUROC 0.60. Across 18,825 buried core variants in 11 independent DMS proteins, ThermoMPNN reaches median AUROC 0.76 (95% CI 0.72–0.78), but for a new protein +1.55 kcal/mol predicts low abundance anywhere from 0.31 to 0.88. So 55/45 stays, and the blot decides.

One binary question sits upstream of every candidate drug.

Caveat 55/45 is an assumed weight. The 11-protein DMS calibration cannot replace it: its new-protein 95% interval (0.31–0.88) spans both branches.
