# Iteration 5 is exploratory: no pre-registered claims

**Standing:** holds for iteration 5 (decided by the user 2026-09-25).

- **Decision.** Iteration 5 runs as one step with no `mva-science hypothesis-add` filings. Its
  methodology entries record question, inputs, commands, output and conclusion, and replace the
  `## Hypothesis and prediction` / `## What would refute it` / `## Verdict` sections with
  `## Positive control` (a known case the method must recover before it is applied to the child)
  and `## Limits`.
- **Why.** Iterations 2 and 4 pre-registered the database-first generators; they came back
  refuted or at chance (H093, H094). The user chose to start from the data — the two alleles and
  the protein — and explore routes the earlier iterations never tried, fast, in one step.
- **What it costs.** No result here is confirmatory. The report says so in its rigor section:
  a route that passes its positive control is a *validated method applied once*, not a tested
  claim. The positive control is the discipline that replaces pre-registration.
- **How to apply.** An entry without a positive control, or whose control failed, reports the
  route as uninterpretable (the lesson of entry 153).
