Step 3 — the reads, with no aligner and no caller

Step 2 found alleles by aligning reads and running two callers over them. Both are models with assumptions — where a read belongs, what a haplotype looks like. This step reads the same bytes with neither: it counts how often each allele's 31-letter spelling appears. Where the two agree the allele has survived a genuinely disjoint instrument; where they disagree, the disagreement is the finding.
