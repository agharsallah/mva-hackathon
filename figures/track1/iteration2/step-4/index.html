Step 4 — every gene, ordered, with nothing filtered out

Iteration 1 used a funnel: rare AND protein-altering AND biallelic AND PASS, and a gene was in or out. This step replaces every one of those cuts with a multiplier. A common allele is divided down, a filtered call is down-weighted, a gene nobody has curated keeps a ratio below 1, and a gene with one damaging allele and no partner is scored at the square root of its evidence. Nothing is removed, so the question "which gene leads" has an answer for all 11,465 of them — and the nulls beside it say how much that answer is worth.
