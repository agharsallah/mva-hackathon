The research record

The research record: what is being asked, what is claimed, what was observed, and what that adds up to. Every claim states what would refute it before it is tested, every observation names the run it came from, and every confidence shows the arithmetic behind it. Nothing here is asserted without a receipt, and what has not been established yet says so.
