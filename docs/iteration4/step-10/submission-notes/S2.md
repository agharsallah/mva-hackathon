## The finding, in one sentence

Iteration 4's four independent generators (walk, twin, PRISM, LINCS) share **one** approved drug. That is what chance gives (null p95 1.0, p 0.263, so **H094 is refuted**). The shared drug, **dasatinib**, passes all three checks (H095 met, weakly): direction, the child's two-source safety, and the screen. It was already a known lead, not a discovery.

## Limitations and what would change the conclusion

- H094 would need two or more shared drugs to clear this null. A larger G2 (a fuller literature extraction) or a G4 with a passing positive control are the realistic ways more agreement could appear.
- Dasatinib's case would change with any of:
  - a K3 test independent of G3 (e.g. PRISM 20Q2 AUC on the aneuploidy split);
  - a callable pharmacogene panel;
  - a paediatric label reading of the growth-retardation reports.
